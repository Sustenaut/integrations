## 0.14.2 - 2026-11-13

Commit `a47adea4b9ea`; release record `pub:sha2-256:cc6zdjv3hakloetr7ma6j6clpmsl6cdxzjqpdbcdjs37r2sgsbgq`.

### Added
- The ingest pod loads the convergence engine's need states: INGEST_NEED_STATES, {"tenant": ..., "dataset": ...} like INGEST_SIGNALS, from inbox/need-states/ into the dataset's need_states table; avrofile has encode_need_states and decode_need_states
- scan.yml takes a runs-on input, the runner label the scan runs on (ubuntu-latest by default), so a repository with self-hosted runners scans on them
- The ingest pod loads the platform's nightly pubkit checks: INGEST_PUBKIT_CHECKS, {"tenant": ..., "dataset": ...} like INGEST_NEED_STATES, from inbox/pubkit-checks/ into the dataset's pubkit_checks table; avrofile has encode_pubkit_checks and decode_pubkit_checks

### Fixed
- The ingest pod scans as soon as it starts, then every two minutes, so a deploy leaves no gap in its reports longer than the restart

## 0.14.1 - 2026-11-06

Commit `548e61b426ad`; release record `pub:sha2-256:pakyty7qef4wu33wogajn5mkaxgdkdenar3yhjj4zifvl25wrxfa`.

### Added
- The service and the ingest pod answer /livez while the process is up; /healthz is integrations.calls.health over their checks: the ingest pod is unready, with 503, once its scanner goes three intervals without finishing a pass, and the service lists a storage buffer it can't reach as an alert only, since a call needs nothing it holds
- The ingest pod loads observability's signals, when INGEST_SIGNALS names their tenant and dataset, from that tenant's inbox/signals/ into the dataset's signals table
- The cloudflare.workers plugin, for Workers deploys
- workers/call-log, a Durable Object per Cloudflare sender that keeps its call records durably and writes its file every two minutes, even an idle one, with caller.js's CallLogSink to send it records

## 0.14.0 - 2026-11-06

Commit `7e3a4604deaf`; release record `pub:sha2-256:pecxorr363weqbmckyftckktf5milea3536l5nlurhc44vyf37ta`.

### Changed
- StorageSink sends its files through the storage client, as storage.api's sink operation: tried once, for up to a minute, and not recorded, as before
- A sink's timer sends its first file a random part of a cycle in, so pods started together don't send at once

### Added
- A registry operation may name its own timeout and tries, in place of its class's
- Storage clients, clients/js/storage.js and integrations.clients.storage, generated from Sustenaut/storage's openapi.json as vendored and pinned in openapi/storage.json, as plugin storage.api
- Unavailable carries the last response, and a Replay answer may give its ETag
- A retry waits a random time up to 2**n s (full jitter) or what a 429's or 503's Retry-After says, up to the class's timeout, and a call never runs past its timeout times its tries, in calls.py and caller.js
